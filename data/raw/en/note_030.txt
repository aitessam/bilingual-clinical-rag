Sample Type / Medical Specialty: Dermatology
Sample Name: Moderate Plaque Psoriasis

CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Worsening scaly skin patches on elbows and knees for six months.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 34-year-old female presents with a six-month history of well-demarcated, scaly, erythematous plaques over both elbows and knees, with associated itching. She reports a family history of psoriasis and recent worsening coinciding with a stressful period at work. No joint pain or stiffness.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY:
No significant past medical history.

MEDICATIONS:
Emollient cream as needed.

ALLERGIES:
No known drug allergies.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION:
Well-demarcated erythematous plaques with silvery scale over extensor surfaces of both elbows and knees, covering approximately 8% of body surface area. Nails show mild pitting. No synovitis or dactylitis.

ASSESSMENT:
Moderate plaque psoriasis, body surface area approximately 8%, with early nail involvement.

PLAN:
Commence topical corticosteroid combined with vitamin D analogue applied once daily to affected areas. Advise regular emollient use. Screen for psoriatic arthritis given nail changes and arrange follow-up in eight weeks to assess response. Discuss lifestyle factors including stress management and refer to dermatology for consideration of phototherapy if inadequate response to topical treatment.
