Sample Type / Medical Specialty: Orthopedics
Sample Name: Ankle Fracture Post-operative Follow-up

CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Routine six-week post-operative review following ankle fracture fixation.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 27-year-old male sustained a bimalleolar right ankle fracture following an inversion injury while playing football, treated with open reduction and internal fixation. He now returns for six-week follow-up, reporting good progress with reduced pain, currently weight-bearing as tolerated in a walking boot.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY:
Previously fit and well, no significant medical history.

MEDICATIONS:
Paracetamol 500 mg as needed.

ALLERGIES:
No known drug allergies.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION:
Surgical wound well healed, no signs of infection. Mild residual swelling around the ankle. Range of motion improving, dorsiflexion to neutral, plantarflexion to 30 degrees. Neurovascularly intact distally.

ASSESSMENT:
Bimalleolar ankle fracture, satisfactory radiological and clinical healing at six weeks post-fixation.

PLAN:
Progress weight-bearing to full as tolerated, wean out of walking boot over the next two weeks. Commence formal physiotherapy for ankle range of motion and strengthening. Repeat weight-bearing radiographs at ten weeks. Advise gradual return to low-impact activity, defer contact sport until at least four months post-surgery.
