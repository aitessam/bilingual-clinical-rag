Sample Type / Medical Specialty: Endocrinology
Sample Name: Hypothyroidism

CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Fatigue, weight gain, and cold intolerance for four months.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 41-year-old female reports a four-month history of progressive fatigue, unintentional weight gain of approximately four kilograms, cold intolerance, and mild constipation. She also notes dry skin and hair thinning. No neck swelling or pain.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY:
No significant past medical history. Family history of autoimmune thyroid disease in her sister.

MEDICATIONS:
None regularly.

ALLERGIES:
No known drug allergies.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION:
Dry skin, mild non-tender diffuse thyroid enlargement, delayed relaxation phase of ankle reflexes. Heart rate 58 bpm regular, blood pressure 118/76 mmHg. No proximal muscle weakness.

ASSESSMENT:
Primary hypothyroidism, likely autoimmune (Hashimoto's thyroiditis) in origin, confirmed biochemically with elevated thyroid-stimulating hormone and low free thyroxine, positive thyroid peroxidase antibodies.

PLAN:
Commence levothyroxine 50 micrograms once daily, taken on an empty stomach. Recheck thyroid function tests in six to eight weeks to guide dose titration. Advise on symptoms of over- and under-replacement to report. Arrange lipid profile given association between hypothyroidism and dyslipidaemia. Continue annual thyroid function monitoring once stable.
