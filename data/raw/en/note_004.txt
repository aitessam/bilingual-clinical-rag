Sample Type / Medical Specialty: Cardiology
Sample Name: Hypertension Management

CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Persistently elevated home blood pressure readings.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 45-year-old female with a five-year history of hypertension attends with home blood pressure readings consistently above 150/95 mmHg despite adherence to a three-drug regimen. She denies headache, visual disturbance, or chest pain. No symptoms suggestive of secondary causes.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY:
Essential hypertension, obesity (BMI 32).

MEDICATIONS:
Amlodipine 10 mg once daily, losartan 100 mg once daily, indapamide 2.5 mg once daily.

ALLERGIES:
Sulfa drugs (hives).

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION:
Blood pressure 156/98 mmHg (repeated, consistent), heart rate 76 bpm. No radiofemoral delay. Heart sounds normal. No abdominal bruits. No peripheral oedema.

ASSESSMENT:
Resistant hypertension, blood pressure uncontrolled on three antihypertensive agents at adequate doses. Secondary causes to be excluded.

PLAN:
Add spironolactone 25 mg once daily as fourth-line agent. Arrange investigations for secondary hypertension including renal ultrasound, plasma aldosterone-to-renin ratio, and screening for obstructive sleep apnoea. Reinforce weight loss and dietary sodium restriction. Review blood pressure and renal function in four weeks.
